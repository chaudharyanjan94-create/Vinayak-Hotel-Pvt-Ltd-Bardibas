Getting here

  • On the East-West Highway at Bardibas Main Chowk, near the junction toward Kathmandu.
  • Janakpur Airport: about 37 km, roughly 50 minutes by car (Trip.com listing).
  • Nearby places named on Trip.com: Memory Park and Panchadhura Temple.

The highway is busy, so rooms facing the road may hear traffic. Ask when booking.
